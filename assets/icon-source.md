# 明音凛头像图标

角色形象与原立绘属于《异环》及其权利方；头像为本工具使用的改编图标。

立绘转载来源：[TapTap：新 S 级明音凛立绘公开](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/840948557765673199)。

制作方式：内置 imagegen，以原立绘为参考制作方形头像；保留金发、粉色眼睛、发夹、蓝紫色蝴蝶结与原画风格。随后仅转换 Windows ICO 格式，包含 16、20、24、32、40、48、64、128、256 像素尺寸。

应用图片：`app-icon.png`；EXE 图标：`app.ico`。

最终制作提示词：

> Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: Windows desktop application icon, square portrait. Input image is the EDIT TARGET: a promotional illustration of Akane Rin / 明音凛 from Neverness to Everness. Make a clean square avatar using only a tight head-and-upper-shoulders crop of this exact illustrated character. Keep her face, pink-magenta eyes, blonde bangs, small smile, dark headband, blue-purple bow and zigzag hairclip faithful to the original drawing and angle. Her head and hair should fill most of the square with a little clear margin, readable as a 32-pixel app icon. Exclude the hand, guitar and full body by framing only. Replace the promotional typography behind the crop with a plain pale periwinkle background. No words, no logos, no border, no new accessories. Do not redesign the face, hair or art style. This is an adaptation of the existing illustration into a recognizable portrait icon, not a new character.
